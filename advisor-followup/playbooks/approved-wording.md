# Approved wording for client communication

Use these as the base for every client-facing draft. Plain, warm, short. No investment advice, no performance claims, no promises about outcomes.

## Tone

- Address the client by first name after "Dear" on first contact, by first name alone afterwards.
- One purpose per email. Say what you need, by when, and how to reach us.
- No jargon. "The form that lets us gather your account details" rather than "KYC documentation".
- Never mention specific products, returns, tax outcomes or guarantees.

## Standard openings

- After a meeting: "Thank you for meeting with me on <date>. As discussed, here are the next steps."
- Requesting a document: "To move forward, we need <document>. You can reply to this email with it attached, or upload it through the client portal."
- Confirming a meeting: "I have scheduled our next meeting for <date and time>. The invitation is attached. Let me know if another time suits you better."

## Standard closings

- "If you have any questions in the meantime, reply to this email or call the office."
- Sign-off: "Kind regards," then the advisor's name and the firm's name.

## Disclaimer (required at the end of every client email)

"This message is for the named recipient and does not constitute investment, tax or legal advice. Please do not send account numbers or passwords by email."

## Things never to write

- Anything that reads as a recommendation to buy, sell or hold.
- Any figure about expected returns or tax savings.
- Any deadline that the firm has not confirmed.
- Any reference to another client.
