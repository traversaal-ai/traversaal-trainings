---
description: Read new approvals, execute approved items, verify open work items against the real system, escalate what has breached its service level, and refresh the index, for the items the runner's role owns. Part of run-cycle; also runs on its own. Argument - optionally "as <name or role>"; with no argument, every role's items (the original behaviour).
---

Run the progress monitor.

1. Read `config.md`, the `team` role, the status board, the ledger, `playbooks/sla.md`, `playbooks/definition-of-done.md` and `playbooks/approval-rules.md`. Note today's date and time. Runner from "as <name or role>"; with no runner named, act for every role and say so.
2. **Read approvals.** Through the `chat` role, read the approvals and operations channels for lines `approve WI-NNNN` or `reject WI-NNNN` posted since the last run. For every work item in Awaiting Approval, also check whether a person changed its `approval` field. Apply each decision per the ledger skill, for any item (bookkeeping): approved becomes `status: Approved` with `approved_by` and `approved_at`; rejected becomes `status: Cancelled` with a History line quoting any reason. A planning request counts only when the planner approves it.
3. **Execute approved items** the runner's role owns. For every such item in Approved, invoke its `owner_agent` to execute. The agent sets In Progress and records `external_ref`.
4. **Verify open items** the runner's role owns. For every such item in In Progress or Blocked (and Approved items executed in step 3), invoke `verifier-agent` to check and update the record. Set `last_checked` on every item looked at, including Awaiting Approval items.
5. **Stop conditions.** For every open item the role owns, if the client meeting it was prepared for has already taken place and the item is not Done, set `status: Blocked` with History "meeting passed" and skip escalation. If `escalations` is already 3, skip escalation.
6. **Escalate.** For every open item the role owns not Done after verification and with `waiting_on: firm` (an item in Awaiting Approval always counts as `firm`, whatever its field says, because it waits on a person here; items waiting on the client, the custodian or a third party follow the "Who we are waiting on" table in `sla.md` instead and never go up the ladder), compare its age and `due` against `playbooks/sla.md`, including the "Before the next meeting" rule (take the household's Next meeting from the status board). Awaiting Approval items use the approval request service level, measured from the request's History line. If breached, send the next escalation on the ladder through the `chat` role with the wording in the playbook, increment `escalations`, append History "Escalation N to <who>". After escalation 3, set `status: Blocked` with History "handed to a person after three escalations".
7. **Regenerate the indexes** in the local build.

Report: runner and role, approvals read (ids and decisions), items executed (ids and external refs), items verified Done (ids and evidence), items escalated (ids, level, who), items blocked and why, items that could not be checked, items found that belong to another role. One line each. If nothing changed, say so.
